Terms of Use

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. What RangeGate is

RangeGate is a deterministic verification tool. It recomputes the statutory mechanics of an Indian transfer pricing study — arm's length range construction under Rule 10CA, multi-year aggregation, Form 3CEB to Local File consistency, Master File and CbCR thresholds, and statutory penalty arithmetic — from figures you supply, and compares its result with what the study declared. Every verdict cites the rule it was computed under. The same input always produces the same output; no language model participates in any verdict.

2. What RangeGate is not

RangeGate is not tax advice, legal advice, or professional advice of any kind, and it is not an audit. It does not source, select, screen or validate comparables, expresses no opinion on benchmarking merit, functional analysis or economic substance, and decides nothing that the law leaves to professional judgement — where two readings are supportable, it reports the question rather than choosing an answer.

RangeGate does not substitute the professional judgement of the Chartered Accountant who signs Form 3CEB. The decision to sign, and the responsibility that attaches to the signature, remain entirely with the person who signs. A RangeGate result — including a run in which every check passes — is a statement about arithmetic mechanics as of the moment it was computed, not an assurance that a study is correct, complete or able to withstand challenge.

RangeGate makes no promise of any outcome with the Indian income-tax authorities or any other authority: no promise that an assessment, audit, adjustment or penalty will or will not occur, and no promise that any authority will accept any position a study takes.

5. Verification records

A Pre-Signature Verification Record, when issued, is an HMAC-signed receipt stating that a verification run with a given verdict summary was executed by a given engine version at a given time and — for records issued from 22 September 2026 — on the input whose SHA-256 fingerprint the signature binds. Records issued before that date do not establish which study was verified. It stores no figure from the study — see the Privacy Policyfor exactly what it contains. Anyone holding a record's hash can authenticate it at /verify. A record authenticates the run; it does not certify the study.
