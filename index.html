Scope of this verification — read it before you rely on it

RangeGate verifies statutory mechanics. It does not verify benchmarking merit.

Comparables and financial data are supplied by the preparer. RangeGate does not source, select or validate any comparable, and expresses no opinion on the merit of the benchmarking analysis or on how the study would fare before a Transfer Pricing Officer.

A result in which every check passes is not an opinion that the study is sound. It means that the arithmetic and the statutory mechanics — Rules 10B, 10CA, 10D, 10DA, 10DB and ss.92C, 92D, 92E — reconcile with the figures that were entered. Whether those figures describe the right set of comparables is a question of professional judgement that stays entirely with the Chartered Accountant who signs.

This notice travels with every result: it is shown on screen, printed in full in the PDF, and included inside the canonical payload that the record's signature is computed over — so a record stripped of its scope notice fails verification.

Six families of checks, every one cited.

A check FAILs only where the statutory arithmetic has exactly one answer. Where two readings are supportable, the ceiling is NEEDS REVIEW carrying both citations — and checks marked as matters of judgement have no path to FAIL at all, enforced in the engine rather than by convention.

Family A

The arm's length range, rebuilt by the statutory position method

Rule 10CA(7) and 10CA(9) do not say "interpolate". They say: the lowest value such that at least 35% of the dataset is at or below it — and if the count is exactly 35%, the mean of that value and the next. Excel's PERCENTILE.INC answers a different question. RangeGate recomputes both and tells you which one your study used.

Rule 10CA(4), (5), (6), (7), (8), (9); proviso to s.92C(2)

Family B

Multi-year margins aggregated, not averaged

Rule 10CA(3) aggregates numerators and denominators across the years — ΣOP over ΣOC. The average of three yearly ratios is a different number, and it is the number most spreadsheets produce. Whether prior-year data was admissible at all stays a matter of judgement, and is reported as one.

Rule 10B(4) provisos; Rule 10CA(2), 10CA(3)

Family C

Form 3CEB against the Local File, joined on transaction IDs

Every transaction reported under s.92E should have a benchmark, and every benchmark should appear in the return. RangeGate joins the two lists on explicit transaction IDs only. It will never fuzzy-match on amount or party name: a near miss invents an orphan that does not exist, and a phantom orphan is a false accusation with a rupee figure attached.

s.92E; Rule 10D; Rule 10D(2)

Family D

Method, tested party and PLI have to agree with each other

The declared margin is recomputed from the tested party financials you supply. RPM without a gross margin and CPM without a cost base are structural failures. A Berry ratio on a manufacturing profile is a question, not a failure — so it is reported as a question.

Rule 10B(1)(b), (c), (e); Rule 10C(2)

Family E

Master File and CbCR thresholds, on this year's numbers

Part A of Form 3CEAA binds every constituent entity with no threshold at all; Part B turns on consolidated group revenue above ₹500 crore together with international transactions above ₹50 crore (₹10 crore for intangibles). CbCR turns on the preceding accounting year's ₹6,400 crore. 3CEAB is the intimation for the Master File; 3CEAC / 3CEAD / 3CEAE are the CbCR chain.

s.92D(4); Rule 10DA; s.286; Rule 10DB

Family F

What the exposure actually is, in rupees

s.271BA is ₹1 lakh for not furnishing the report. s.271AA is 2% of the value of each transaction. s.271G is 2%. s.270A is 50% or 200% of the tax on the adjustment — and the tax rate is shown as a labelled assumption, never as a figure RangeGate pretends to know about your company.

ss.271BA, 271AA, 271G, 270A, 271GB

Questions a CA actually asks.

Do you need our comparables data?

No — and we could not accept it even if you offered. RangeGate never sources, caches or redistributes comparables. You already licensed your database; you paste the resulting margins as numbers. No company names are asked for, because the mechanics do not depend on identity. That inversion is what keeps this product on the right side of your data licence and ours.

Does this replace the CA's judgement?

It replaces none of it. The signature, the opinion and the liability stay exactly where the statute puts them. What RangeGate replaces is the second manual pass over arithmetic — the pass where the reviewer opens the same spreadsheet as the preparer and inherits the same error. A check that recomputes independently catches what a second reading of the same file cannot.

Do I have to pay to get the signed record?

No, and there is no way to pay even if you wanted to: RangeGate has no checkout, no card field and takes no payment anywhere. During pre-launch the signed Pre-Signature Verification Record is issued from the free anonymous check, with no account, at the free tier's rate — ten runs an hour from one network address, which is also what caps the records. When paid tiers open, the pricing section will say so before anyone is asked for a card — it will not happen quietly behind a button you already clicked.

Is an all-PASS result a clean bill of health?

No, and we will not let it be read as one. An all-PASS record means the mechanics of Rules 10CA, 10B and s.92E reconcile with the figures supplied. It says nothing about whether the comparables were well chosen, whether the set is sufficient, or whether the study would survive a Transfer Pricing Officer's view of the economics. That distinction is printed on the result screen, printed in the PDF, and cryptographically bound into the record's signature.

Is there an LLM deciding these verdicts?

There is no model anywhere near a verdict. The engine is a pure rules function: same input, same output, forever, with the rule cited next to each answer. A verdict you cannot reproduce is a verdict you cannot defend in a hearing.

What happens when the input is not enough to compute something?

The check comes back UNMATCHED with the reason, and the checks that depended on it come back UNMATCHED too. It is never a PASS (which reads as safety) and never a FAIL (which reads as an accusation). Missing a transaction value produces no exposure figure at all — never a false ₹0.

Which assessment years are supported?

AY 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27, because the tolerance band under the proviso to s.92C(2) is notified annually and the thresholds have been amended over time. An unsupported year is refused outright with the list of supported years. Computing an old year with today's constants would produce verdicts that are legally wrong while looking perfectly confident.
